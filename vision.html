Why Naijy Exists

Nigeria runs on hundreds of disconnected systems — one way to report a bad road, another to find a doctor, another to trade produce, another to check what government is doing with public money. Most Nigerians have no single place to turn to for any of it.

Naijy exists to fix that: one national digital engine, organised by the sectors that actually make up Nigerian life — security, business, health, agriculture, governance, family, and more — so that whatever a Nigerian needs, there's a platform already built for it.

How It's Built

Every one of the 117 platforms sits inside one of 17 sectors, so nothing is scattered. On top of that sit two smaller groups with a bigger job: the Special Platforms that power the system itself, and the Strategic Platforms built for national-level coordination — things like tracking government revenue, spending, and infrastructure in the open.

All of it runs on one shared login, one shared design, and one shared mission — so using Naijy never feels like jumping between 117 different websites.

Where We're Headed

  1. Community Stewardship
    Handing day-to-day care of individual platforms to Nigerians who are already passionate about that sector — so no platform sits neglected.
  2. Depth Over Width
    Now that all 117 platforms are live, the focus shifts to making each one genuinely useful — real usage, real listings, real reports — not just more platforms.
  3. The Diaspora Bridge
    Making it just as easy for a Nigerian in London, Houston, or Dubai to invest in, sponsor, or stay connected to home through Naijy as it is for someone in Lagos.
  4. Government & Institutional Partnership
    Working with state governments, universities, hospitals, and agencies so platforms like GomentWatch, RevenueMap, and HealthPro are powered by real official data, not just citizen reports.
  5. A Model Other Nations Can Learn From
    Proving, in Nigeria first, that a country can run its own connected digital infrastructure — one that other nations could eventually adapt for themselves.

What This Means for You
