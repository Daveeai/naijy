Verify Your Account

Wallet and airtime features require a quick, one-time verification. This is what keeps Naijy free of fake accounts.
1. Verify Phone Number
One phone number = one Naijy account.
2. Enter Code
Sent via SMS, valid for 10 minutes.
3. Identity Verification (BVN)
Required to activate your wallet. Confirms you're a real, unique person — required by Nigerian financial regulation for wallet services.
Your BVN is sent directly to our licensed payment partner for verification and is never stored on Naijy's servers — only a verified/not-verified result is kept.
4. Choose Your Public Display Name
This is what other users see — it does not have to be your legal name.
